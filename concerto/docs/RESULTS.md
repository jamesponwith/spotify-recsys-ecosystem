## No policy makes a hot ticket cheap

Across all 180 parameter cells, no arm gets the average fan in below 1.2x face.
What a policy chooses is not the price. It is who gets in, and who keeps the difference.

- **Queue as it ships:** fan pays $221 per ticket
  (2.33x face), of which $19 reaches a broker.
  Brokers take 61.1% of the house.
- **Affinity + identity-bound:** fan pays $121
  (1.27x), brokers take 0.0%, and
  96% of superfan demand is served against
  20% under the queue.
- **Market clearing:** fan pays $291 (3.06x) --
  *more* than the queue it replaces -- brokers take 0.0%, the artist
  receives $229 per seat against $95, and
  0.0% of bottom-income-quartile demand is served.
